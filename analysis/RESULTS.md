# Results of the pre-registered test plan

Snapshot of 2026-10-12. Every number here was regenerated by the commands in
`README.md`; the artifacts (git-ignored) are under `outputs/<directory>`.
Intervals are 95% grouped bootstrap: static data resample configurations,
UAV data resample flight × tag × 10 s blocks. "Dev" is the
train+validation pool. "Test" is the grouped test subset, evaluated exactly
once (`final_test/`).

## Recorded deviations and assumptions

- UAV mount sign fixed to the static convention: `(offset, sign)` and
  `(offset+180°, −sign)` are not distinguishable from PDoA alone. The user
  confirmed the node was face-up and aligned with the nose.
- R3's 0.37 m lateral lever arm is not physical (the node is on the centre
  line), so R3 is not a mount estimate.
- R4 is post hoc: the window was chosen after seeing the drift.
- Height and acquisition session are confounded in the static campaign by
  design.
